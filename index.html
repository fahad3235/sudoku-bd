How to play

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9. Tap a cell, then tap a number. Use Notes for pencil marks. Three mistakes end the game.

Keyboard: 1–9 enter · Backspace erase · Arrows move · N notes · U undo · H hint
